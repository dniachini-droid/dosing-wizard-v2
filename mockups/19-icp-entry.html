Thirty-odd elements from a lab, entered together.

A lab panel is not a reading. It is a multi-element result that arrives as one document on one date, and the app has nowhere to put it — a reading holds one parameter from a closed list. This screen shows what the surface would be; the record behind it does not exist yet. Recorded as gap 13.

Date on the report

A lab report carries a date, not a time. That is recorded honestly as a date and the app does not invent an hour for it.

Kept with the panel. Two labs disagreeing is a real thing and the app should not average them into one story.
