Still inside your range

Eleven readings back, this is the lowest of them — but every one of them has been inside 8.6 to 9.2, and the run has been drifting gently down all month rather than moving.

Why it is built this way

A number alone does not say whether 8.68 is the lowest in a fortnight or the middle of a steady run. The new value is drawn onto its own recent history, and the value counts up as the line draws to it, so the reading arrives with its background already there.

One progress value drives all three. The chart draw, the travelling dot and the counting number are read from a single number between 0 and 1. That is the fix, and it is the reason the moment is worth porting rather than describing: three separate animations — a stroke dash, a motion path and a timer — could never stay in step, which is why the dot used to run ahead of its own line.

Every one of those figures is a token. None of them was chosen here.

The register it is written in

Deliberately calm. A reading outside its band gets a useful line rather than an alarm, and one inside gets acknowledgement rather than confetti. The band pulses once, at nine per cent opacity rising to twenty-six and back — confirmation, not celebration.

V1’s splash burst is not carried across. The arrival is the part worth keeping; the confetti is decoration without function.

Where the chemistry is

Nowhere in it. The component is given a list of values, a range and a colour, and it draws a line through them and moves a dot along it. The sentence underneath — “still inside your range” — is the engine’s, rendered here, not worked out here.

V1’s version of this moment computed its own verdict inside the presentation component. That part is deliberately left behind.
