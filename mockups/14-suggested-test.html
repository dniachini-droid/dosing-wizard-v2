Why Friday

You changed the alkalinity dose at 08:20 this morning, from 9.0 to 9.3 mL/day. Friday morning is the earliest a reading can carry any information about whether that did what it was meant to — before then the tank has not had long enough for the difference to show above the noise in an alkalinity kit.
