Working out a rate needs the exact gap between two measurements. A date on its own cannot give that, and the app will not pick a time within the day to make it work — not noon, not midnight, not your usual testing hour.

This cannot be upgraded. Editing the entry will not let you add a time to it. If you genuinely establish when it was — from a photograph, a note, a message — that is a new record carrying the proof, not a change to this one.

What you can do with it

  • ✎ Correct a typo Fixes the value or the date. The original is kept alongside, and any assessment that already used it is left exactly as it was — a fresh assessment is made instead. ›
  • ↻ Replace it with a better record Marks this one superseded and points at the replacement. Both stay visible. Use this when you have a genuinely better record of the same test — not when a repeat test simply gave a different number. ›
  • ⁈ Mark it suspect Says it is plausible but odd enough to want confirming. It stays in view and in your history. It may hold back a dose change until it is settled. It does not become invalid on its own. ›
  • ✗ Mark it invalid Says it is not a usable measurement. It is kept forever and still shown in your history, greyed, and it is excluded from every calculation. You must say why. ›
None of these deletes anything. There is no delete.
