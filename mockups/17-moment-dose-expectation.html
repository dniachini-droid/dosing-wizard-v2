The moment after you record a dose change

The expectation, stated up front

Reload the page to watch it again.

The strongest case in the gap list, because the object already exists. A dose change is a prediction as much as an action: it says the tank should move a certain way over a certain time. The engine already produces that prediction as a fixed, unchangeable record at the moment of the change. PR #6 had the object and no surface for it. This is the surface.

Why state it before the reading arrives

Because then the next test either confirms it or it does not, rather than being read from scratch. Without this, a keeper looks at Saturday’s number with no memory of what they were expecting, and any number at all looks reasonable.

The figure is fixed at the moment the change is recorded and is never revised afterwards. If Saturday comes in at 8.60, the app does not quietly adjust what it said it expected. That is the entire value of the object.

What this screen must never do

Work any of it out. The expected value, the daily movement, the retest date and the destination are all read from the record the engine wrote. This screen formats them.

V1’s version calculated its own expectation inside the popup. That is the part left behind; the timing and the shape are the part carried across.
