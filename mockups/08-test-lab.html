Why this screen exists. V1’s entry form had a dropdown, so recording six tests meant six round trips through a select. This is the fix and it is the whole idea: every parameter is a row, you type the value and press Log, and you move down the page. It is a checklist, not a form.

Something other than a test

  • ↑ A dose change With the date and the time it took effect, because that is the moment everything after it is measured from. ›
  • ◫ An ICP panel Thirty-odd elements from a lab, entered together and checked against reference. ›
  • □ A water change, or a chore Including one that was never on the schedule. ›
  • ⚠ Something went wrong A doser that stopped, a spill, a correction you made by hand. The engine needs to know, and it will not guess. ›
Three routes to the same reading, on purpose. A reading can be logged here, from a due item on Today, or from a parameter’s own detail after tapping its card. They write the same record. Going to another tab to type one number was V1’s most repeated friction and it does not come back.
