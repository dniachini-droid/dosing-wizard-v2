The two on 18 August

They were 12 minutes apart, so they are one testing episode, not two pieces of evidence. The engine used the middle of them — 8.79 dKH at 07:40 — and recorded that they differed by 0.02 dKH, comfortably inside the 0.20 limit that would have flagged the pair as inconsistent.

Why 15 August was left out

It has a date but no time of day. Working out a rate needs the exact gap between two measurements, and a date alone cannot give that. The reading is not deleted, not downgraded and not assigned a made-up time such as noon or midnight. It stays in your history, it can still set your current position if it is ever the most recent thing you have, and it stays date-only for good.

What’s certain enough to act on −0.0198 dKH/day

The measured rate of 0.0400 dKH/day includes some amount of measurement noise. The engine subtracts a fixed multiple of the rate’s uncertainty and acts only on what is left. If nothing is left, it does not act at all.

  • The rate you measured0.0400 Size only; the direction is handled separately.
  • Subtract for uncertainty−0.0202 1.28 × 0.0158 dKH/day. The 1.28 is fixed and is recorded with the result so a replay can prove which value ran.
  • Short by−0.0198 Signed back to falling. This is the only number allowed to size a dose change.
This figure is deliberately smaller than the truth. It is used for one thing and one thing only — sizing the maintenance dose. It is never used to say how much your tank consumes, never used to forecast when you will cross a boundary, and never used to say where you are now. Those all use the full measured rate.

How the dose figure was reached seven numbers

These are seven different quantities. They are kept apart on purpose: collapsing any two of them into one number is how a dosing engine starts lying to you.

  • 1 · What’s running now9.0 mL/day. Your last confirmed pump setting. currentDoseMlPerDay
  • 2 · The dose that would exactly match what your tank uses9.58 mL/day. 0.6576 ÷ 0.0686. Shown so you can see the gap — the engine does not jump to it. maintenanceEstimateMlPerDay
