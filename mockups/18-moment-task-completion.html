What it is telling you, and what it is not

It is telling you that you do this every nine days and that you set it to every seven. That is the one thing the app knows and you do not carry in your head.

It is not telling you that nine is wrong, or that seven was right, or that you should do better. There is no praise, no streak to break, no score. If nine days is what actually happens, that is a fact about your week, and the useful response might be to change the schedule to nine rather than to feel behind on seven.

Worth showing without comment. The restraint is the design.
