The app cannot put this in millilitres

What is missing: how strong your alkalinity solution is. The recipe behind it — which chemical, and how many grams per litre — has never been recorded for this tank.

Why that stops the number: every millilitre has to be worth some amount of dKH before millilitres mean anything. Without that, the app knows exactly how much alkalinity your tank is short of, and has no way to say how much liquid that is. It will not guess a strength, and there is no assumed default.
