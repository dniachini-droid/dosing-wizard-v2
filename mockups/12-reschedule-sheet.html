One sheet, not three. V1 built this once and opened it from both the calendar and the reminder list, so a task could be moved from wherever you happened to be looking at it. That is the whole design and it is worth keeping.

What each of those does
Mark it done todayNext one falls a week from todaynot a week from when it was due
Push it a dayOnly this one movesthe rhythm underneath is untouched
Skip this oneRecorded as skippednot as done, and not silently forgotten
Turn this task offStops scheduling itits history stays
Skip is not done. They produce different histories and the difference matters: a run of skips is a signal that the interval is wrong, and folding them into completions would hide exactly that.
