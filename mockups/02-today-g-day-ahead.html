There is no assessment on this screen, and there will not be. An assessment is a statement about how the tank is right now. The app cannot know what Friday’s reading will say, and a card that looks like a prediction and is not one has no business existing.
What is deliberately absent. No alkalinity card, no dose recommendation, no trend, no “expected” figure. Only the assessment’s prediction object, if a dose change had just been made, could honestly appear on a future day — and that lives on its own moment screen where it is plainly labelled as a prediction.
