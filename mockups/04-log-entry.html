About times

Every form below asks how well you know the time, and none of them fills it in for you. There are four honest answers and they are not equally useful — but a weaker one is always better than a made-up strong one.

Open questions for the owner. (1) “Suspect” can hold back a dose change, but nothing defines what settles it or which screen does the settling — gap 9. (2) Water changes, corrections and delivery problems have no correction path at all: only readings can be superseded or marked invalid, so a mistyped water change cannot be fixed without rewriting history — gap 8. (3) The “not sure” answer on the dose-change form above cannot actually be stored: a dose change’s origin is a closed choice of two, with no way to say it is not established — gap 12.
