A one-off nobody planned

These are the things the engine cannot work out for itself and will not guess at. Each of them changes how a later reading should be read.

  • ↑ I changed the dose With the date and the time it took effect. ›
  • ⚠ The doser stopped, or ran wrong From when to when, and roughly what went in instead. Without it the app reads a gap in dosing as the tank suddenly consuming more. ›
  • ⚖ I corrected it by hand A one-off volume, outside the daily dose. It is a separate kind of event and it is not folded into your maintenance rate. ›
  • ✎ Something else worth remembering A note against a date. It is recorded and it is never used as evidence for anything. ›
