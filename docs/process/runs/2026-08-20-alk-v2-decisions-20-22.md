## The decisions

### Decision 20 — `D_established` is two distinct quantities

`ALK-DELIVERY-RATE-BASIS-001`, new, in `ALK-022`:

```text
D_current  = the alkalinity delivery rate the doser is CONFIGURED to be
             delivering AT THE TIME OF THE RECOMMENDATION
D_history  = the TIME-WEIGHTED MEAN alkalinity delivery rate actually
             delivered ACROSS THE TREND INTERVAL under consideration
```

`ALK-HIGH-BREACH-SAFETY-SIZING-001` is amended to
`D_safety,temp = max(0, D_current − R_down / P_selected)`.
`ALK-CONSUMPTION-ESTIMATE-001`'s previously unnamed interval `D` is named `D_history`.

**Unknown handling refuses.** Unknown `D_current` ⇒ no rate, no executable command, and
**not** `0 mL/day`; the measured state and the reason are surfaced and doser configuration
is requested through the **existing** anomaly/confirmation machinery. Unavailable
`D_history` ⇒ consumption `UNRESOLVED` under the existing handling. The two unknowns are
independent in both directions.

Closes `OI-DELIVERYRATEBASIS-001` (raised as `D-ESTABLISHED-UNDEFINED`).

### Decision 21 — advisory ceiling and floor

`ALK-ADVISORY-RANGE-BOUNDARY-001`, new, in `ALK-003A`:

```text
AdvisoryCeiling = OuterMax + 1.0 dKH        # 12.0 for the owner's configured bounds
AdvisoryFloor   = OuterMin - 1.0 dKH        # 6.0
```

Evaluated on the **resolved episode value**, after `ALK-EPISODE-RESOLUTION-001` and
**before** high-breach sizing. Inclusive at the boundary, under
`ALK-DECIMAL-THRESHOLD-001`'s exact decimal comparison, with no epsilon. At or beyond it:
no dose recommendation, no temporary safety rate, no executable command — and **not zero**.
An escalation output states the measured value, that it is outside the range the engine will
advise on, that the reading should be confirmed by a second test, that the doser should be
checked for fault or overdose, and that correction at this level requires experienced
judgement about the specific system. Outer-bound classification and shortened retesting are
preserved; the maintenance estimate stays `UNRESOLVED`. A contested episode all of whose
members are beyond the boundary escalates rather than being withheld as contested.

**Narrows `OI-SIZINGFLAT-001`. Does not close it**, and no branch boundary was moved.

### Decision 22 — uncomputable consumption under high breach

`ALK-HIGH-BREACH-UNCOMPUTABLE-CONSUMPTION-001`, new, in `ALK-003A`. Where
`OuterMax < A_now < AdvisoryCeiling` and `C_estimate` cannot be computed at all, the state
takes the branch B pathway and sizes from `D_current`, with the maintenance estimate
`UNRESOLVED` and the reason surfaced. Distinct from an unknown `D_current`, which refuses
under decision 20. The three predicates A, B and B′ are asserted **jointly exhaustive and
mutually exclusive** below `AdvisoryCeiling` (`INV-G12`).

Closes `OI-UNCOMPUTABLEC-001` (raised as `SIZING-NO-BRANCH-FOR-UNCOMPUTABLE-C`).
`OI-CZERODISCONT-001` is **not addressed** and remains open.

## Focused review, and the fix pass it forced

`canon-conformance-auditor` and `breaker` were run against the encoding, scoped strictly to
decisions 20–22 and their direct interaction with the safety rails, episode resolution and
retest behaviour. The auditor's registered subagent type is read-only without `Bash`; it was
run under its roster definition as a general-purpose agent and **did** read the diff, by
`git diff 65c6030..HEAD`, and ran the gate read-only. Its findings are verified, not
unverified.

**The headline finding was that the gate did not test the canon.** Every one of decisions 20,
21 and 22 could be reverted *in the canon* with `validate-freeze-5.py` still reporting
`0 checks failed` — the breaker reverted all three simultaneously and the gate stayed green.
Section 17 recomputed **fixtures against fixtures** and touched the canon only through a few
string-presence assertions, so canon-versus-fixture divergence was undetectable in either
direction. The canon is the sole behavioural authority and is what an implementer reads.

Section 18 is the answer: a canon-side twin for every fixture-side check, each naming the
reversion it exists to catch. The mutation suite grew from 9 to **30**, and every one now
fails the gate.

Three further gate defects the mutations found, each after a check had been written and
believed:

| Blind spot | Why it was blind |
|---|---|
| `_units` treated a whole JSON file as one paragraph | `adversarial.json` holds one blank line in 185 KB, so unrelated history markers elsewhere exempted the entire corpus — and a live `D_established` in `AD-SAF-007`'s note passed. A JSON file has no paragraphs; the unit is now the line. |
| the sized rate was asserted on one named case only | every other non-escalating case was free to state any number. A fixture stating a **99 mL/day increase** at 11.4 dKH passed both the gate and the recorder with the record digest unchanged. Every non-escalating case is now recomputed. |
| the uninterpretable disjunct was asserted against the prose | deleting it from the partition **code block** left the explanatory paragraph intact and the check passed. It is now asserted against the block. |

And two recorder defects:

- the recorder reached **none** of `AD-ESC-001/002/003`, `AD-SAF-009` or `AD-DHS-003`, and
  still reported "0 unreadable" — `unreadable` was only appended for a *known* key whose
  inputs were missing, so a stated value in a shape it never looked for was invisible rather
  than reported. Coverage now includes the boundaries, `R_down`, the sized rates, the
  actuator commands and the low-breach correction volume: **420** values over **50**
  fixtures, up from 348 over 43;
- the recorder's own `or`-chained lookup treated a `D_history` of **exactly zero** — the
  doser off for the whole interval, precisely the state that accompanies a high breach after
