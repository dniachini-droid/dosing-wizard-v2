## Stage 1 — admissibility

**This run edits `docs/canon/`, which `/implement-chemistry` step 7 otherwise
forbids.** That is the whole point of the task and it is admissible for one
reason only: `ALK_V2_FREEZE_4`'s own reopening rule provides for exactly this,
and the owner invoked it.

> Any future Alk behavioural or load-bearing completeness change requires: exact
> affected rule IDs; concrete failure scenario; canon vs implementation
> classification; affected coverage fixture updates; `IMPACTS_FROZEN_ALK` where
> caused by a shared change; Alk V2 Freeze 5 or later.
> — canon, `PART III — ALK V2 FREEZE 4 DECLARATION`, *Reopening rule*

Each requirement is met by this run: the affected rule IDs are enumerated in the
Freeze-5 declaration; the concrete failure scenario for every decision is the one
already recorded in `ALK-V2-OPEN-ISSUES.md`; the classification is
`CANON_DEFECT` + `OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED` in each case; the coverage fixtures
are updated in this commit; no shared rule body changed, so
`IMPACTS_FROZEN_ALK` does not arise.

The other admissibility test in step 1 — *every rule this change implements must
be stated in current frozen canon* — is inverted here. This run is the mechanism
by which rules **become** canon. The applicable constraint instead is the one in
`CLAUDE.md`: adopted chemistry may not arrive by invention. It arrives here by
**owner decision through a governed reissue**, which is the only path
`CLAUDE.md` recognises for a rule with no prior freeze.

## Owner decisions 16–19 — third owner pass

The owner supplied four further decisions, resolving the six findings the focused review of
F5-13/14/15 escalated. They are part of `ALK_V2_FREEZE_5` and **supersede the earlier
Freeze-5 wording wherever they conflict**. No new numeric constant is introduced by any of

### Decision 16 — high-breach safety sizing

`ALK-HIGH-BREACH-SAFETY-SIZING-001`, new, in `ALK-003A`:

```text
R_down        = min(A_now - A_safe,high, 0.50)
D_safety,temp = max(0, D_established - R_down / P_selected)
```

Negative-consumption materiality owns the maintenance model, not the delivered safety rate.
Superseded: `ALK-HIGH-BREACH-UNRESOLVED-001`'s automatic pause to 0 mL/day, and
`ALK-HIGH-BREACH-NO-PAUSE-001`'s HOLD of the established delivery rate — both preserved in
place as marked history rather than deleted. Zero is now a floor. `WG-ALK-051`, in canon and
as a fixture, changes from "pause to 0" to `2.784992784992785 mL/day` (`10.0 − 0.50/0.0693`),
rounding to `2.8`.

### Decisions 17 and 19 — the canonical testing episode

`ALK-TESTING-EPISODE-001` (`ALK-005B`), `ALK-EPISODE-RESOLUTION-001` (`ALK-005C`) and
`ALK-EPISODE-SINGLE-OUTPUT-001` (`ALK-005D`). Membership is the episode — explicit repeat
relationship, otherwise the **existing** 30-minute window — not exact timestamp equality.
Same-method members pool; incompatible-method members are kept distinct and the episode is
`CONTESTED_METHODS`. Every consumer — position, outer-bound classification, `SAFETY_RETURN`
triggering, rapid basis, selection, trajectory, consumption, forecast, maintenance — reads
the one resolved episode output. A contested latest episode withholds the affected inference
and requests `REPEAT_NOW`, and is never replaced by an older episode.

`ALK-SAME-TIMESTAMP-COALESCE-001` is amended to the selection-side consequence; its original
mechanics are preserved as a quoted superseded block. `ALK-010` and `ALK-RAPID-BASIS-001`
carry the consumer clauses.

**Recorded exposure.** No rule governs outer-bound classification from a contested episode,
so one straddling `OuterMax` withholds the classification and requests an immediate repeat.
A worst-case-member rule would be a new owner decision and is deliberately not created.

### Decision 18 — repeat-spread domain and exact decimals

`ALK-REPEAT-SPREAD-DOMAIN-001` (in `ALK-005`) confines the 0.20 dKH threshold to same-method
repeats and canonises two `NOT_RUN` states rather than inventing a cross-method threshold.
`ALK-DECIMAL-THRESHOLD-001` (`ALK-005E`) gives the canonical threshold predicates exact
decimal semantics — no pre-rounding, no epsilon — so `0.20 > 0.20` is false. Scope is
`ALK-005`'s spread, `ALK-004`'s range edges and `ALK-003A`'s outer-bound and completion
comparisons; every derived quantity stays binary64 under the determinism contract.
`ALK-V2-IMPLEMENTATION-CONTRACT.md` §7 gains item 5a; `OI-DETERMINISM-001` and
`OI-BOUNDARIES-001` are amended to match.
